**Education.** I was born in Taiwan and now based in Nagoya, Japan. I received M.S. and Ph.D. from the Graduate School of Informatics, Nagoya University in 2021 and 2024, respectively. I received B.S. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from National Taiwan University in 2018.

**My research interest** focuses on speech processing, with a main focus on all subfields around speech synthesis: speech quality assessment, voice conversion, text-to-speech, and neural vocoding. I also worked on real-world applications including speaking-aid and accent conversion.

**Honors and activities.** I was honored the 16th IEEE Signal Processing Society Japan Student Best Paper Award, the Best Paper Award at the 13th Asia Pacific Signal and Information Processing Association Annual Summit and Conference (APSIPA ASC), 2021 and Best Student Paper Award at the 11th International Symposium on Chinese Spoken Language Processing (ISCSLP), 2018. I received the Research Fellowship for Young Scientists (DC1) from Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS), which last from April 2021 to March 2024. I was honored the Outstanding Graduate Student Award (学術奨励賞) of Nagoya University in 2023. I am a founder of the [VoiceMOS Challenge series](https://sites.google.com/view/voicemos-challenge/) and a co-organizer of the [Voice Conversion Challenge series](http://www.vc-challenge.org/). I am also a reviewer of several journals including IEEE SPL, IEEE/ACM TASLP, Speech Communication, CSL, etc.

**Work experience.** During my MS/PhD, I worked as an intern or a student researcher at Google Japan, FAIR (Fundamental AI Research) at Meta, Reality Labs Research at Meta, and NTT Communication Science Laboratories. I also work closely with the Institute of Information Science in Academia Sinica, Taiwan with Prof. Hsin-Min Wang and Prof. Yu Tsao, where I was an undergrad research assistant.
